## Design Context

### Users
Miami Beach Fire Department firefighters and the Ladder 1 apparatus-planning
team use this application to assign required equipment to physical apparatus
compartments, review placement progress, collaborate across devices, and export
the resulting plan. It must work quickly with touch, mouse, and keyboard input
on phones, tablets, laptops, and desktop workstations.

### Brand Personality
Operational, dependable, and direct. Preserve the recognizable MBFD
fire-service identity and the existing red/navy visual language without turning
the application into a decorative marketing experience.

### Aesthetic Direction
A compact, high-clarity apparatus planning tool. Controls should feel sturdy
and obvious, information density should remain useful on large screens, and
phone/tablet layouts should adapt for touch rather than merely shrink.

### Design Principles

1. Make every primary workflow reliable with touch, mouse, and keyboard.
2. Preserve all important capabilities across mobile, tablet, and desktop.
3. Favor clear equipment names, quantities, statuses, and recovery messages over
   decoration.
4. Use at least 44 by 44 CSS pixel touch targets for interactive controls.
5. Protect shared planning data from accidental loss, stale overwrites, and
   silent synchronization failures.
